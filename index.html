Meet the Black Soldier Fly (Hermetia illucens) — a harmless, non-pest insect that does not bite or carry diseases. Their larvae act as nature's ultimate recyclers, rapidly converting organic food waste into high-quality protein and nutrient-rich fertilizer!

About Our Mission

Waste Management Leaders

We are a dedicated team operating at the Asian University for Women's (AUW) Black Soldier Fly Research Centre.

By utilizing insect-based bioconversion, we treat organic waste on-site, eliminating municipal landfill tipping and reducing harmful methane emissions.
